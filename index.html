Delivery Information

Choose home delivery or pickup based on your location.

🏠 Home Delivery

Your order can be delivered to your selected delivery location.

  • 📍 Delivery area will be collected during ordering.
  • 📏 Home delivery is available within 20 km from My Home Bakery.
  • 🎂 For locations more than 20 km away, home delivery is available only when the order contains a cake of 3 kg or above.
  • 🏪 If the above condition is not satisfied, Pickup / Self Collection is available.
  • 🚚 Delivery tracking is available when the order is out for delivery.
  • 📦 You can track your order using your Track ID.

🏪 Pickup / Self Collection

You can also collect your order directly from My Home Bakery.
